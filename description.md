Celebrates every sale on your **PayNow** store: the pill shows who bought what, with their avatar and the price, and the glow plays an animation.

Five animations (**Confetti**, **Gold**, **Rainbow**, **Fireworks**, **Cash**), a bigger show for big sales, a reminder in the pill until you have looked, and a tab with today's, this month's and all-time takings and the latest sales. Needs a PayNow API key that can read orders; it is stored encrypted. Needs Notch 0.12 or newer.
